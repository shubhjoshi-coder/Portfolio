About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student currently in my 3rd semester at Parul University, Gujarat. Before this, I completed my Diploma in Computer Engineering, which gave me my first real exposure to programming and the way software is built.

Right now, I'm focused on strengthening my fundamentals — learning HTML, CSS, C and C++ in depth, while slowly stepping into UI/UX design. I don't have years of professional experience; what I do have is consistent curiosity and a habit of building small things to understand how they work.

This portfolio itself is part of that process — a Frontend Foundations and Interactive Designing assignment built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, to show what I've learned so far.
